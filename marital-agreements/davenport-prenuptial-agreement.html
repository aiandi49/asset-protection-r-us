The four provisions that shaped the entire negotiation

Separate property

The Davenport estate stays untouched

The family home, investment holdings, jewelry, and collections remain solely Davenport property, now and permanently — as does everything already belonging to Khloe.

Marital property

A one-way split

Anything Liam earns or builds during the marriage becomes 50% Khloe's from the first dollar. Growth in Khloe's existing assets, however, remains entirely her own.

Spousal support

Waived in one direction only

Liam gives up any right to request spousal support. Khloe's right to request it from him is fully preserved.

Primary residence

Awarded regardless of contribution

The marital home goes to Khloe outright in a divorce, independent of who purchased it or paid for its upkeep.

Article I

Purpose and intent

This agreement is entered into in contemplation of the marriage of Liam Vance ("Prospective Husband") and Khloe Davenport ("Prospective Wife"). Its stated purpose is to define, in advance, the ownership and division of assets belonging to each party, and to protect the property, holdings, and financial interests of the Davenport family in the event the marriage ends for any reason.

Article II

Disclosure of assets

The Prospective Wife and her family have disclosed the following as subject to protection under this agreement:

  • The Davenport family residence and all associated investment properties
  • The Davenport stock and securities portfolio
  • Personal property including jewelry and collected valuables
  • Khloe Davenport's trust fund and anticipated future inheritance
  • The condominium purchased for Khloe following her graduation

The Prospective Husband's assets and income are not itemized in this draft beyond his stated occupation.

Article III

Separate property

All property listed in Article II, along with any future appreciation in its value, shall remain the sole and separate property of the Davenport family and of Khloe Davenport individually. This designation is permanent, surviving the marriage regardless of its duration or the circumstances of its end.

In the original story, this article catalogued each item so precisely — down to a specific jewelry collection and a set of antique clocks — that it read less like a legal safeguard and more like a message about whose world was being protected.

Article IV

Marital property and division of assets

Any income, business, or asset generated by the Prospective Husband during the course of the marriage shall be deemed marital property, of which fifty percent (50%) shall belong to the Prospective Wife immediately upon acquisition. Appreciation in the value of the Prospective Wife's separate property, by contrast, shall remain entirely her own and shall not be subject to division.

No reciprocal claim is granted to the Prospective Husband over the Prospective Wife's separate assets or their appreciation.

Article V

Spousal support

The Prospective Husband hereby waives, fully and irrevocably, any right to seek spousal support or alimony from the Prospective Wife under any circumstance. This waiver does not apply in reverse; the Prospective Wife's right to seek spousal support from the Prospective Husband remains fully intact and unaffected by this agreement.

Article VI

Primary residence

In the event of divorce or separation, sole right, title, and possession of the couple's primary residence shall be awarded to the Prospective Wife, irrespective of which party purchased the property, holds title to it, or has contributed financially to its acquisition or maintenance.

Article VII

Business ventures during marriage

Any business enterprise founded, joined, or invested in by the Prospective Husband during the marriage shall be treated as marital property under the terms of Article IV, subject to the fifty percent division described therein, regardless of the Prospective Husband's sole labor, expertise, or initial capital contribution.

Article VIII

Amendment, severability & governing law

This agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both parties and countersigned by Henderson & Klein LLP. Should any provision be held unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall continue in full force. This agreement shall be governed by the laws of the state in which it is executed, with disputes subject to arbitration administered by Henderson & Klein LLP.

Article IX

Acknowledgment

Each party acknowledges that they have had the opportunity to review this agreement, that they enter into it freely and without duress, and that they have been advised to seek independent legal counsel prior to signing.

Signatures

This sample is unsigned and non-binding. A real prenuptial agreement should be signed only after independent review by each party's own attorney.
