Key terms, at a glance

The four provisions the Crofts never expected to matter

Asset waiver

She signs away her claims — not him

Isabella waives any and all present or future claims to Ethan's personal and business assets. No matching waiver is written for his claims to hers.

Consideration

A loan guarantee, in exchange

In return, Ethan agrees to personally guarantee a portion of the Croft family's outstanding debt on a real-estate venture.

No disclosure required

Nobody itemized what "his assets" meant

The agreement never requires either side to list, verify, or even ask what Ethan's business and personal holdings actually were.

Hidden trigger

A separation clause, disguised as a favor

Signing this document simultaneously satisfies a separate, unread condition tied to the dissolution of the marriage.

Article I

Purpose and intent

This agreement is entered into by Ethan Sterling ("Husband") and Isabella Croft Sterling ("Wife"), five years into their marriage, at the request of the Croft family and in connection with a personal loan guarantee sought on behalf of Isabella's brother. It is understood by the parties to formalize the couple's financial arrangements going forward and to provide security to the Croft family in the event of a future separation.

Article II

Disclosure of assets

The parties acknowledge the marital home, vehicles, club memberships, art, and jewelry currently enjoyed by the household. This agreement does not itemize the legal ownership of these assets, require documentation of title, or verify which entity — personal, marital, or otherwise — holds them.

No inventory of Ethan Sterling's business interests, holdings, or trust arrangements is attached to or required by this agreement.

Article III

Waiver of claims

Isabella Croft Sterling hereby waives, fully and irrevocably, any and all present or future claims to the personal and business assets of Ethan Sterling, howsoever held, including any entity, trust, or company in which he holds an interest, in the event of a separation or dissolution of the marriage.

Article IV

Consideration

In exchange for the waiver described in Article III, Ethan Sterling agrees to personally guarantee a portion of the outstanding debt held by the Croft family in connection with a real estate venture undertaken by Liam Croft.

Article V

No reciprocal waiver

This agreement does not require Ethan Sterling to waive any claim to the assets of Isabella Croft Sterling or her family. No mirror provision protecting her interests was drafted or requested.

This is the clause nobody thought to write. The agreement was built to take everything from one side — so no one considered what needed protecting on the other, or asked what "Ethan Sterling's assets" might actually turn out to include.

Article VI

Effect on trust-held property

Any property held in trust or through a business entity in Ethan Sterling's name — including, without limitation, real property, vehicles, and personal effects — is governed by the terms of its own separate trust instrument and is not addressed by this agreement. This agreement does not establish, and should not be read to imply, that any such property constitutes marital property.

Article VII

Termination and separation

Execution of this agreement, together with the concurrent loan guarantee described in Article IV, constitutes full satisfaction of the separation condition contemplated between the parties. The parties acknowledge that signing gives effect to the dissolution of the marriage.

Article VIII

Amendment, severability & governing law

This agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both parties. Should any provision be held unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall continue in full force. This agreement shall be governed by the laws of the state in which it is executed.

Article IX

Acknowledgment

Each party acknowledges that they have had the opportunity to review this agreement and that they have been advised to seek independent legal counsel prior to signing. Isabella Croft Sterling acknowledges she signed under the belief that this agreement would compel Ethan Sterling to surrender his assets to her family.

Signatures

This sample is unsigned and non-binding. A real postnuptial agreement should be signed only after independent review by each party's own attorney.
