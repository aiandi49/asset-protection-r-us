Key terms, at a glance

The four provisions she signed without reading

Premarital holdings

Corporate holdings stay solely his

Ethan's corporate holdings, equity shares, intellectual property, and affiliated business interests — all acquired before the marriage — remain his sole property, untouched by the marriage.

The residence

Held in trust, outside the marriage

The marital home sits inside a trust structure that predates the marriage. On paper, that means it was never jointly owned — even after years of living there together.

Personal income

Each spouse keeps their own

Vanessa retains her personal assets and the income she earns during the marriage. That part of the agreement was never in dispute.

The signing itself

A five-minute meeting, called "a formality"

Nothing in the document was hidden. What was missing was time, explanation, and anyone slowing the room down long enough for her to actually read it.

Article I

Purpose and intent

This agreement is entered into in contemplation of the marriage of Ethan Cole ("Prospective Husband") and Vanessa Carter ("Prospective Wife"). Its purpose is to define, in advance, the ownership and division of assets held by each party prior to the marriage, and to establish which property will remain separate regardless of the length or outcome of the marriage.

Article II

Disclosure of assets

The Prospective Husband discloses the following as premarital property subject to protection under this agreement:

  • Corporate holdings and equity shares held prior to the marriage
  • Intellectual property developed prior to the marriage
  • Affiliated business interests and related entities
  • The marital residence, held under a trust structure established before the marriage

This disclosure is technically complete. It does not, however, include a plain-language summary, a valuation of these holdings, or any requirement that the Prospective Wife's own counsel review it before signing.

Article III

Separate property

All property listed in Article II, together with any future appreciation in its value, shall remain the sole and separate property of Ethan Cole. This designation survives the marriage regardless of its duration and is not affected by any joint use, joint decision-making, or joint appearance of ownership during the marriage.

This is the paragraph she signed without reading. Nothing in it was hidden — the corporate holdings, the trust, the residence were all named in plain sight. What was missing wasn't disclosure. It was a conversation.

Article IV

Marital residence

The marital residence is held under a trust structure predating the marriage and is not subject to division upon separation or divorce, regardless of either party's financial or personal contribution to its selection, furnishing, or upkeep during the marriage.

Article V

Personal income and assets during marriage

Each party shall retain sole ownership of their own personal income, earnings, and assets acquired during the marriage through their own individual effort. Neither party's personal earnings become jointly owned property by virtue of the marriage alone.

Article VI

Effect upon dissolution

In the event of divorce, this agreement shall govern the division of assets as follows: the Prospective Wife retains her personal assets and income; any property, holdings, or corporate structures belonging to the Prospective Husband under Articles II through IV remain entirely separate and are not subject to division.

Article VII

Signing acknowledgment

Both parties acknowledge that they have had the opportunity to seek independent legal counsel prior to signing. This agreement does not require proof that either party exercised that opportunity, nor does it require a waiting period between presentation of the document and signature.

Article VIII

Amendment, severability & governing law

This agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both parties. Should any provision be held unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall continue in full force. This agreement shall be governed by the laws of the state in which it is executed.

Article IX

Acknowledgment

Each party acknowledges that they have reviewed this agreement and enter into it freely. The Prospective Wife's signature below reflects her agreement to these terms as presented, whether or not she read each provision in full before signing.

Signatures

This sample is unsigned and non-binding. A real prenuptial agreement should be signed only after independent review by each party's own attorney.
