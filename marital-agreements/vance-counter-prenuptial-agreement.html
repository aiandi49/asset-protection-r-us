Article I

Purpose and intent

This agreement is entered into in contemplation of the marriage of Liam Vance ("Prospective Husband") and Khloe Davenport ("Prospective Wife"). It is offered as a direct counterpart to an earlier draft proposed on the Prospective Wife's behalf, mirroring its structure and its clauses — applied equally, in both directions, to both parties. Its purpose is to protect what each spouse brings into the marriage while sharing fairly in what they build together.

Article II

Disclosure of assets

In the interest of full transparency, both parties disclose the following as subject to this agreement:

  • The Prospective Husband's architectural practice and its ongoing projects
  • The Prospective Husband's family holdings and inheritance, including interests in Vance Corporation
  • The Prospective Husband's personal investment portfolio
  • The Prospective Wife's trust fund and anticipated future inheritance
  • The Prospective Wife's condominium and personal property

Both parties affirm that this disclosure reflects a complete and shared understanding of the assets each brings into the marriage.

Article III

Separate property

All property listed in Article II, along with any future appreciation in its value, shall remain the sole and separate property of whichever party owned it before the marriage. This protection applies equally: the Prospective Husband's holdings are shielded exactly as the Prospective Wife's are, with no distinction in how either estate is treated.

This article is deliberately a mirror. Every protection the original draft gave one family, this version gives both people — because a partnership only works when the same rules apply to everyone in it.

Article IV

Marital property and division of assets

Any income, business, or asset generated jointly by either party during the course of the marriage shall be deemed marital property, divided equally between both spouses. Appreciation in the value of each party's separate property shall remain their own individually, with the same standard applied without exception to either spouse.

Where one spouse's separate assets contribute directly to the marriage's shared standard of living, that contribution shall be acknowledged in any future division, regardless of which spouse made it.

Article V

Spousal support

Neither party waives the right to seek spousal support. Should the marriage end, either spouse may request support, calculated according to the length of the marriage, each party's financial standing at the time, and their contributions — financial and otherwise — to the household and to one another's careers.

Article VI

Primary residence

In the event of divorce or separation, the disposition of the couple's primary residence shall be determined by each party's financial and non-financial contribution to its purchase and upkeep. Whichever spouse does not retain the residence shall receive a guaranteed minimum settlement reflecting their share, ensuring neither party is left without recourse.

Article VII

Business ventures during marriage

Any business enterprise founded, joined, or invested in by either party during the marriage shall be evaluated on its own terms — accounting for each spouse's labor, expertise, and capital contribution — rather than defaulting to an automatic division that ignores who actually built it.

Article VIII

Amendment, severability & governing law

This agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both parties. Should any provision be held unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall continue in full force. This agreement shall be governed by the laws of the state in which it is executed, with disputes subject to neutral, mutually agreed arbitration rather than arbitration controlled by either party's own firm.

Article IX

Acknowledgment

Each party acknowledges that they have had the opportunity to review this agreement, that they enter into it freely and without duress, and that they have been advised to seek independent legal counsel prior to signing. This offer stands regardless of either family's approval, and is extended in good faith as the foundation for an honest partnership.

Signatures

This sample is unsigned and non-binding. A real prenuptial agreement should be signed only after independent review by each party's own attorney.
