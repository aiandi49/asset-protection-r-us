This Is Not a Law Firm

Nothing on this site is legal advice, and nothing here is a lawyer. Every document in these four sections is an educational mock-up — a realistic starting draft meant to teach you what a real version of that document usually contains and why. None of it is drafted for your specific situation, none of it is state-specific unless noted, and none of it should be signed, filed, or relied on as-is.

The actual way to use this: read the mock-up, understand the shape of it, fill in your own numbers and entities where it invites you to, then bring it to a licensed attorney in your state to turn it into the real thing. That's the entire point — showing up to that first meeting already understanding the document, instead of hearing about it for the first time in an expensive hour.

How To Actually Use This

Four steps, in order

  • Open the mock-up closest to your situation and read it end to end — every clause is explained in plain language, not just legal boilerplate.
  • Download it and fill in your own names, entities, and numbers where it invites you to. This turns it from a generic example into a real first draft of your situation.
  • Bring that filled-in draft to a licensed attorney in your state as the starting point for your first meeting — you'll spend that hour on your actual questions instead of on the basics.
  • Let the attorney turn it into the real, binding version. Nothing on this site should be signed, filed, or relied upon before that step happens.
