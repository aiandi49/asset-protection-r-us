Article I

Purpose and intent

This letter accompanies the estate of Richard Carter and sets out the Grantor's instructions regarding the Carter Family Trust as they relate to his son, Marcus Carter. Its purpose is to preserve family assets across a difficult personal transition, and to ensure those assets remain legally and structurally separate from any marital estate, without requiring any agreement between Marcus and his spouse.

Article II

Trust assets

The following are held within the Carter Family Trust, and not in Marcus Carter's individual name:

  • The aircraft known as the Celestine, together with its hangar and related equipment
  • Shares in Carter Aviation and affiliated aviation partnerships
  • Commercial real estate holdings
  • Investment accounts and related business interests

Title to each of these assets remains with the Trust at all times, both before and after distribution to the beneficiary.

Article III

Timing of disclosure

The Grantor directs that this letter, and the accompanying estate summary, be withheld from Marcus Carter until such time as his marriage reaches the point of dissolution, at the Trustee's discretion. This timing reflects the Grantor's stated wish that his son not carry the weight of this inheritance while still deciding the future of his marriage.

This is the clause that made everything else possible. Not concealment for its own sake — a father trying to make sure his son was chosen for who he was, before he ever knew what he stood to inherit.

Article IV

Separate property designation

Because the assets described in Article II were held in trust and neither received, disclosed, nor commingled with marital funds or property during the course of Marcus Carter's marriage, they are treated as his separate property under the applicable law of the jurisdiction, regardless of when he becomes aware of them.

Article V

Conditions of distribution

Ownership of the trust assets carries responsibilities including insurance, maintenance, aviation management, and continued oversight of the family trust structure. The Trustee shall provide Marcus Carter with counsel and support sufficient to manage these responsibilities, and shall not permit full distribution to occur without adequate review of the estate.

Article VI

No obligation to disclose

Marcus Carter is under no obligation, legal or otherwise, to disclose the existence of the Trust or its assets to any party, including a spouse or former spouse, prior to or during the process of separation or divorce. This letter does not require, and does not recommend, any particular course of disclosure; that decision belongs to the beneficiary alone.

Article VII

Personal letter from the Grantor

Accompanying this instruction is a personal letter from Richard Carter to his son, addressing matters beyond the estate itself. The Trustee shall deliver this letter unopened and unread, as its contents are intended for Marcus Carter alone.

Article VIII

Trustee authority & governing law

The Trustee retains full discretion to interpret and carry out these instructions in keeping with the Grantor's intent. This letter and the trust it accompanies are governed by the laws of the jurisdiction in which the Trust was established.

Article IX

Acknowledgment

Marcus Carter acknowledges receipt of this letter, the accompanying estate summary, and the personal letter from his father, and confirms that he has had the opportunity to review these materials with independent counsel before making any decisions regarding disclosure, distribution, or use of the trust assets.
